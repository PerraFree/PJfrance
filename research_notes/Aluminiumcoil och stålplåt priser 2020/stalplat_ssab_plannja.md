# Prepainted galvanized steel sheet (SSAB GreenCoat / Plannja) and underlying raw-material prices, 2020 – Sept 2026

**Research date:** 2026-09-29. **Method note:** WebSearch worked; almost every WebFetch (ssab.com, cision, MEPS, Fastmarkets, Eurometal, Argus, S&P, tradingeconomics, World Bank, USGS, Lindab, Plannja, Byggahus, Plåtgrossisten, etc.) was egress-blocked from this sandbox, so figures below come from search-result snippets of those sources. Every number is tagged as either **[DATA]** (quoted from a source) or **[EST]** (my estimate from the data points, to be treated as approximate). EUR/SEK conversion factors used, as instructed: 2020 10.5; 2021 10.1; 2022 10.6; 2023 11.5; 2024 11.4; 2025 11.0; 2026 11.0.

**Weight basis used for SEK/kg conversions (0.5–0.6 mm prepainted steel):** 0.5 mm × 7 850 kg/m³ ≈ 3.9 kg/m² bare steel, ≈ 4.1 kg/m² incl. zinc (Z275) + paint; 0.6 mm ≈ 4.7 kg/m² bare, ≈ 4.9 kg/m² coated. These are engineering constants, not sourced market data.

## Key question 1 — Northern Europe HRC price EUR/t: yearly averages 2020–2026, the 2022 peak and the 2020 trough

### Takeaway
Northern European HRC went from a pandemic trough of roughly €380–420/t (Q2 2020) to an all-time record above €1 370/t in March–April 2022, fell back to €650–680/t by end-2022, drifted to a cycle low of ~€530–570/t in late 2024/mid-2025, and has recovered to ~€735–745/t in September 2026 on the back of the new EU import regime (quota −47 %, 50 % out-of-quota duty from 1 July 2026). Full-year averages are only partly published in accessible sources; the yearly table below is therefore an estimate built from documented spot points.

### Cited Findings
- 2020 trough: Southern European domestic HRC was assessed at €380–395/t ex-works on 13 May 2020, with Italian deals heard at €380–390/t — [Fastmarkets Europe HRC wrap, May 2020](https://www.fastmarkets.com/article/4009257/europe-hrc-wrap-domestic-prices-fall-further-eu-mills-seek-export-deals). Southern European HRC "remained almost flat at €400 per ton, with trades concluded in the €390–400 per ton range" in mid-2020 — [Eurometal "Steel HRC prices in Europe hit bottom"](https://eurometal.net/steel-hrc-prices-in-europe-hit-bottom-sources-say/). (Northern Europe typically traded €10–30/t above Southern Europe; see EST below.)
- 2021 boom: Northern European HRC reached "their highest ever level on March 25, 2021 at €830/mt ex-works, up from the start of the year by €195/mt" — [S&P Global Platts, 25 Mar 2021](https://www.spglobal.com/platts/en/market-insights/latest-news/metals/032521-feature-northern-european-hrc-prices-reach-record-high-platts-data). Lindab noted that "the price of European sheet metal had increased by over 40 percent since the beginning of November [2020]" when it raised prices in March 2021 — [Fastighetstidningen/DI](https://fastighetstidningen.se/lindab-skruvar-upp-priser-pa-stal-och-aluminiumprodukter-di/).
- Annual average change: "In the European market, the average annual price variation in 2021 was 110%… the variation in the annual average prices was negative in 2022 and 2023… In 2024… a timid recovery" — [PricePedia, April 2024](https://www.pricepedia.it/en/magazine/article/2024/04/15/march-2024-hot-rolled-coils-prices-update/).
- 2022 record: "Fastmarkets calculated its daily steel hot-rolled coil index for Northern Europe at €1,374.46 per tonne on March 16, 2022, which were the highest prices on records going back to 2003" — [Eurometal / Fastmarkets, March 2022](https://eurometal.net/european-hrc-prices-driven-higher-by-restocking-amid-concerns-over-imports/). (The peak continued into April 2022; see EST.)
- End-2022: transaction levels in Northern Europe in the week to 23 Dec 2022 were €650–670/t EXW, some sources saying €680/t was achievable — [Eurometal, Dec 2022](https://eurometal.net/european-hrc-prices-edge-higher-mills-eye-further-increases-for-2023/).
- 2023: a Northern European mill indicated €680–700/t ex-works in May 2023 — [Eurometal, May 2023](https://eurometal.net/european-hrc-buyers-resist-price-rises-amid-weak-demand/).
- Late 2024: "for November–December 2024 lead times, prices were still around €550–570 per tonne ex-works"; Q1 2025 offers €580–600/t — [Eurometal "HRC prices flat in Europe; mills hoping for rebound in 2025"](https://eurometal.net/hrc-prices-flat-in-europe-mills-hoping-for-rebound-in-2025/).
- 2025: Fastmarkets Northern Europe HRC index €593.75/t on 4 Feb 2025 — [Eurometal, Feb 2025](https://eurometal.net/steel-hrc-prices-inch-upward-across-europe/); "the average value of northwest EU HRC deals captured by Argus in June [2025] was €533/t" — [Argus](https://www.argusmedia.com/en/news-and-insights/latest-market-news/2717393-arcelormittal-increases-nw-eu-hrc-offer-to-eu610-t); Fastmarkets index ~€601/t in mid-July 2025 — [Eurometal](https://eurometal.net/eu-hrc-market-sees-dip-as-temporary/).
- 2026: "European HRC prices remained stable at approximately €630–€640 per tonne ex-works in Northern Europe as of January 20, 2026" — [SMM](https://news.metal.com/newscontent/103736886). ArcelorMittal raised its NW-EU HRC offer to €700/t in early 2026 — [GMK Center](https://gmk.center/en/news/arcelormittal-raises-prices-for-hot-rolled-coils-to-e700-t-in-northern-europe/). Week ending 11 Sept 2026: Northern Europe HRC "estimated €742 per tonne ex-works" — [openPR weekly tracker](https://www.openpr.com/news/4643060/european-steel-prices-2026-hrc-rebar-and-crc-weekly-price). Late Sept 2026: "domestic HRC prices in Northwest Europe and Italy held stable at 734 and 736 EUR/tonne EXW", buyers waiting for the 1 Oct quota reset — [SMM, Sept 2026](https://news.metal.com/newscontent/104139652-smm-steel-european-hrc-shrouded-in-wait-and-see-sentiment-buyers-await-oct-1-quota-reset-as-market-holds-flat).
- A search snippet attributed to MEPS said "European average HRC price was 476 EUR/t in 2022 and 951 EUR/t in 2023" — [MEPS sample PDF](https://insights.mepsinternational.com/hubfs/MEPS%20Steel%20Price%20Outlook%202024%20(Sample).pdf). **This is clearly garbled/mis-attributed (2022 was the record year, 2023 far lower) and should NOT be used.**

## Key question 2 — Hot-dip galvanized (HDG) coil EUR/t 2020–2026, and prepainted/organic-coated coil if published

### Takeaway
HDG in Northern Europe tracked HRC with a premium of roughly €100–200/t: ~€1 200–1 350/t delivered in 2021, ~€720–740/t in 2023, ~€650–680/t in 2024, €710–835/t in 2025–26, with ArcelorMittal asking €780/t in 2026. No public series for prepainted/organic-coated coil was found; Fastmarkets noted coated-steel prices and margins hit record highs in 2021–22.

## Key question 3 — LME zinc USD/t yearly averages 2020–2026

### Takeaway
USGS/IMF data give LME zinc cash averages of ≈$3 005/t (2021), $3 486/t (2022), $2 648/t (2023), $2 778/t (2024) and ≈$2 866/t (2025); 2026 has rallied sharply — $4 037/t on 9 Sept 2026 (highest since April 2022) and $3 860/t on 28 Sept 2026. 2020 (~$2 270/t) is from memory, not a retrieved source.

### Cited Findings
- USGS Mineral Commodity Summaries: LME cash zinc averaged 136.3 ¢/lb in 2021, 158.1 ¢/lb in 2022, 120.1 ¢/lb in 2023, 126.0 ¢/lb in 2024 — [USGS MCS 2026 zinc](https://pubs.usgs.gov/periodicals/mcs2026/mcs2026-zinc.pdf); 2025 average 130 ¢/lb (USGS 2026 estimate) — same source. Conversion ×22.046: 2021 $3 005/t; 2022 $3 486/t; 2023 $2 648/t; 2024 $2 778/t; 2025 $2 866/t.
- IMF global zinc price 2025: $2 866.60/t — [FRED PZINCUSDA](https://fred.stlouisfed.org/series/PZINCUSDA). Statista: 2024 average $2 776/t — [Statista](https://www.statista.com/statistics/242291/global-zinc-price/).
- 2024 intra-year: zinc rose 13 % from $2 621/t to $2 979/t by year-end — [INN 2024 year-end review](https://investingnews.com/daily/resource-investing/base-metals-investing/zinc-investing/zinc-price-update/).
- 2025: LME 3-month started 2025 at $3 127/t (2 Jan), high $3 412 (29 Jan) — [INN H1 2025](https://investingnews.com/daily/resource-investing/base-metals-investing/zinc-investing/zinc-forecast/).
- 2026: "Zinc fell to 3,859.85 USD/T on September 28, 2026"; earlier "held above $3,800 per tonne, remaining close to the $4,037 reached on September 9th, its highest level since April 2022, reflecting tightening supply" — [Trading Economics zinc](https://tradingeconomics.com/commodity/zinc).
- Australian government (2022) forecast LME zinc to average ~$3 600–3 750/t in 2022 — [Resources and Energy Quarterly, Sept 2022](https://www.industry.gov.au/sites/default/files/minisite/static/8f915ab4-c06d-41cf-98ee-5bc628230bdc/resources-and-energy-quarterly-september-2022/documents/Resources-and-Energy-Quarterly-September-2022-Zinc.pdf).

### Inferences
- Table (USD/t, LME cash annual average): 2020 ≈ 2 270 **[EST, from memory of USGS MCS 2022: ~103 ¢/lb]**; 2021 3 005; 2022 3 486; 2023 2 648; 2024 2 778; 2025 2 866; 2026 YTD ≈ 3 300–3 500 **[EST]** (Jan ~$2 900 → Sept ~$3 900–4 000).
- Zinc's contribution to HDG cost is small: Z275 coating ≈ 275 g/m² both sides ≈ 0.07 kg zinc per kg of 0.5 mm steel → at $3 500/t ≈ $0.25/kg steel ≈ €0.22/kg ≈ 2–3 SEK/kg-equivalent per m²... i.e. roughly €20–25/t of sheet; zinc explains only a minor part of HDG price moves, the HRC base explains most.

### Gaps
- 2020 annual average not retrieved from a live source (USGS/Westmetall/World Bank pages all egress-blocked).
- No monthly 2026 series retrieved beyond the two September 2026 points.

## Key question 4 — SSAB Europe average realized prices / price development 2020–2026 (annual & quarterly reports); GreenCoat surcharges

### Takeaway
SSAB does not publish an explicit EUR/t or SEK/t realized price for SSAB Europe; it reports revenue, shipments and the percentage effect of prices on revenue. The documented arc: record earnings 2021–2022 "driven by high steel prices", revenue −13 % in 2024 "mainly related to lower prices in SSAB Europe and SSAB Americas", 2025 revenue −6.9 % with "lower prices" a −4 pp effect and SSAB Europe loss-making in Q3 2025, then a price upturn in Q1–Q2 2026 (Europe EBIT ~six-fold to SEK 624 m in Q2 2026) ahead of the new EU trade regime. No public GreenCoat-specific surcharge amounts (energy 2022, CBAM 2026) were found; SSAB's stated green premium for SSAB Zero/fossil-free steel is ~$325/t.

## Key question 6 — Key events: 2021 boom, 2022 energy crisis, EU safeguards, Oct-2025 tariff/quota proposal, CBAM from Jan 2026, US Section 232, and what happened in 2026

### Takeaway
The steel price cycle was driven by: post-COVID restocking and supply shortage (2021, +110 % annual average), the Ukraine war/energy shock (record €1 374+/t HRC March–April 2022, then a collapse as demand fell), a long 2023–2025 slump with record import penetration and US 50 % Section 232 tariffs (June 2025), and then a regulatory turn: CBAM definitive phase from 1 Jan 2026 (certificate €75.36/tCO₂ in Q1 2026, small net effect in 2026), and Regulation (EU) 2026/1384 from 1 July 2026 replacing the safeguard with a 47 % smaller quota (18.35 Mt) and 50 % out-of-quota duty — which has lifted Northern Europe HRC from ~€630 in January to ~€740/t in September 2026.
